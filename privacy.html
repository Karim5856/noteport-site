Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Applies to the NotePort Chrome extension and the website noteport.dev.

In short: NotePort does not collect, store, or sell any of your data. Your notes travel from your browser to Obsidian on your own computer, and nowhere else.

What NotePort does with your data

NotePort reads the Studio Notes you choose to export from a NotebookLM page open in your browser, converts them to Markdown, and sends them to the Obsidian Local REST API running on your own computer, so they are saved in your vault.

This happens entirely inside your browser and between your browser and your own machine. The developer of NotePort never receives your notes, your notebooks, or any information about how you use the extension.

What is stored on your device

NotePort saves your settings in your browser’s extension storage. This includes:

  • the address and API key for your Obsidian Local REST API connection
  • your export preferences, such as how to handle duplicate notes

These settings stay in your browser. You can remove them at any time by clearing the extension’s data or uninstalling it.

What NotePort does not do

  • It does not collect personal information, analytics, or usage statistics.
  • It does not use cookies or trackers, and it does not show ads.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties, because it has no data to share.
  • It does not run any remote code or load scripts from external servers.
  • It does not read pages other than NotebookLM.

Why NotePort needs its permissions

  • Access to NotebookLM pages: so it can read the Studio Notes you select for export.
  • Connection to your local Obsidian: so it can send the exported notes to the Local REST API on your computer (localhost).
  • Storage: so it can remember your settings between sessions.

Use of data

The data NotePort handles is used only to provide its single purpose: exporting your notes to your Obsidian vault. It is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or any other purpose, and it is never transferred to anyone.

Children

NotePort is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

This website

The noteport.dev website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, cookies, forms, or third-party scripts. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs, which are covered by GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date. If a change affects how your data is handled, it will be announced in the extension’s release notes.

Contact
